# Compliance Notes

This document is not legal advice.

shandong is currently positioned as a research, historical backtesting, risk observation, and simulated trading tool. It is not an investment adviser, broker, or asset manager.

## Current Positioning

Current versions should only provide:

- Educational research tools.
- Historical data analysis.
- Technical indicators.
- Backtesting.
- Risk observation.
- Simulated or paper trading.

Current versions must not provide:

- Real broker connectivity.
- Real-money order placement.
- Automatic trading.
- Managed accounts.
- Guaranteed returns.
- Personalized buy or sell recommendations.

## Required Disclaimer

All public pages, reports, exports, and dashboards should show a clear disclaimer:

```text
本系统仅用于学习、研究和模拟交易，不构成投资建议。历史回测不代表未来收益。
```

## Regulatory Risk Areas

If the product later charges users for individual stock analysis, forecasts, buy/sell advice, trading signals, automatic trading, or managed portfolios, it may involve regulatory requirements such as:

- Securities investment advisory rules.
- Investment adviser licensing.
- Broker-dealer or broker API requirements.
- Programmatic trading rules.
- Data licensing and redistribution rules.
- Marketing and performance advertising rules.
- Privacy and cybersecurity obligations.

These areas require legal and compliance review before implementation or commercialization.

## Explicit Prohibitions

The product must not:

- Guarantee returns.
- Use stock-picking marketing language.
- Present research outputs as certain future predictions.
- Automatically trade on behalf of users.
- Collect broker passwords.
- Store broker API keys or secrets in current stages.
- Exaggerate AI capabilities.
- Claim that backtests prove future profits.
- Connect to real trading before compliance review.

## Commercial Communication Rules

Marketing should focus on:

- Research workflow.
- Risk awareness.
- Backtesting education.
- Paper trading practice.
- Data organization.

Marketing should avoid:

- "Guaranteed profit."
- "Buy this stock now."
- "AI knows tomorrow's price."
- "Automatic wealth machine."
- "No-risk trading."

## Future Live Trading Review

Before any real broker interface or live trading capability is considered, the project should complete:

- Legal review.
- Broker API terms review.
- User suitability and disclosure review.
- Security review for credentials.
- Audit logging design.
- Manual risk controls.
- Incident response planning.

Until those reviews are complete, the product should remain a research and simulation platform only.
