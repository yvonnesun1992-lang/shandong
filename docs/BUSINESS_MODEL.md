# Business Model

shandong should first grow as a subscription-based research and paper-trading platform. It should not become a real-money trading or stock-picking product before compliance review.

## Recommended Direction

The recommended path is:

1. Build a useful research tool.
2. Add stable data workflows and reports.
3. Add paper trading and simulated portfolios.
4. Commercialize as a subscription research platform.
5. Consider real brokerage connectivity only after legal and compliance review.

## Free Plan

### Target Users

- Beginners learning quantitative research.
- Users who want to try the workflow.
- Users evaluating the dashboard.

### Features

- Small number of stock trend scores.
- Basic indicators.
- Sample data experience.
- Basic dashboard.
- Limited backtest examples.

### Limits

- Limited watchlist size.
- Limited backtest range.
- No team accounts.
- No API.
- No real trading.

## Pro Plan

### Target Users

- Individual investors using the product as a research workflow.
- Users who want more backtesting and watchlist tools.

### Features

- Larger watchlists.
- Full historical backtests.
- Watchlist management.
- Risk reminders.
- Report export.
- Paper trading account.
- More data cache capacity.

### Boundaries

- Research and simulation only.
- No personalized investment advice.
- No automatic trading.
- No guaranteed returns.

## Advanced Plan

### Target Users

- Power users.
- Small teams.
- Research groups.

### Features

- Multi-strategy management.
- Portfolio backtesting.
- Advanced data cache.
- Team accounts.
- Research API.
- Private deployment option.
- Custom report templates.

### Boundaries

- No broker integration unless compliance review is complete.
- No paid buy or sell signals.
- No managed portfolio service.

## Future Enterprise / Private Deployment

This can be considered after the core platform is stable.

Potential features:

- Private deployment.
- Custom data connectors.
- Internal research dashboards.
- Role-based permissions.
- Audit logs.

This still must respect data licensing, privacy, and securities regulations.

## What Not To Sell Now

Do not sell:

- Guaranteed returns.
- Stock recommendations.
- Real-time buy/sell signals.
- Automated real-money trading.
- Broker credential storage.
- Managed investment services.

## Current Priority

The current priority should be:

```text
Subscription research tool + paper trading platform.
```

The product should not be positioned as:

```text
Real trading software, stock recommendation software, or guaranteed-profit AI software.
```
